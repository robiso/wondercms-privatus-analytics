# Changelog

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-30

- The extra tracker modules are unticked until the owner ticks them.
- Listed from the WonderCMS fork robiso/wondercms-privatus-analytics.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-28

- Initial release.
- Adds the Privatus Analytics tracking script to the head of every page.
- Settings in the Menu tab for the site ID and for each tracker module (engage, auto, vitals, clicks, ask).
- Your own visits are not counted while you are logged in, unless you turn that off.
